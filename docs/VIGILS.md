# Vigils, Lumens and rewards: design + brainstorm

## The loop
1. **Move.** Today's movement becomes *pledgeable* distance (1 km = 60 ward, or 1 mi ≈ 97).
2. **Go there.** Stand within 150 m of a landmark: GPS on the phone, or Demo GPS in the preview.
3. **Act.**
   - **Fortify** your clan's vigil: pledge distance to raise its ward (max 1,000).
   - **Siege** a rival's vigil: pledge distance to drain its ward. At 0 it **falls**. If your clan has a **Vigil Seal**, you claim it instantly; otherwise it lies open.
   - **Establish** an open vigil: costs a Seal and starts at 200 ward, plus anything you pledge.
4. **Hold.** Every day, each vigil pays **tribute** (✦ Lumens) to every member of the holding clan, and grants a **boon** to the whole clan, until someone breaks it.
5. **Decay.** Ward fades 8% a day, so a vigil nobody visits slowly goes dark. Clans have to keep moving to keep their ground.

### How Awakening ties in
A clan that **awakens** (full weekly gauge plus 60% of kin Bonded) gets:
- the Aspect Power for 2 weeks,
- **+2 Vigil Seals**, the only free way to claim ground,
- **empowered vigils**: half decay and ×1.5 tribute while the Power lasts,
- +150 essence and +100 ✦ for every member.

So: move together → awaken → claim landmarks → the whole clan earns daily rewards → rivals who move more can take them → move again.

## Lumens ✦ (in-game premium currency)
**Earned** only by playing: vigil tribute and Awakenings. In the prototype nothing is bought with real money.

**Spent** in the shop:
| Item | ✦ | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Vigil Seal | 400 | Claim ground without waiting for an Awakening |
| Ward Shield | 250 | Protect your weakest vigil from sieges for 24 h |
| Rally Horn | 120 | A second Rally today |
| Spirit auras (Aurora, Starlit, Obsidian, Sakura) | 300 | Cosmetic glow on your spirit companion |

## Reward brainstorm (next ideas)
**In-game**
- **Relics:** each landmark drops a unique collectible after 7 straight days held (a "Needle Shard", a "Troll's Hubcap").
- **Spirit skins** unlocked by holding a vigil of your own Aspect for a full season.
- **Clan banners** that fly from every vigil the clan holds, designed by the clan.
- **Night vigils:** some landmarks pay double tribute between sunset and midnight (safety-gated to busy, lit places).
- **Weather events:** "Atmospheric river" weekends where every pledge counts ×1.5 if you move in the rain.
- **Seasons:** at season end, the clan holding the most landmark-days wins a permanent monument on the map.
- **Personal stewardship:** the member who pledged most to a vigil this week is its *Keeper* and gets a title shown on the beacon.

**Real-world (needs partners; design so the game never requires spending)**
- Local partners sponsor a landmark: holding clans get a coffee or smoothie discount nearby.
- Parks and trail organizations: trail-maintenance volunteer days give big ward bonuses.
- Gear shops: season-end rewards for top clans.
- Charity mode: every 1,000 ward pledged citywide funds a tree planting.

## Fairness and safety rules (for the real build)
- Vigil actions need verified presence (GPS) **and** movement that day, so you can't sit at home and siege.
- Plausibility checks on GPS speed; Demo GPS never exists in production.
- Landmarks are public, busy, well-lit places. No vigils on private property, schools or roads.
- No exact player locations are shown to other clans; beacons show clan ownership only.
- Caps on how many vigils one clan can hold, so new clans always have ground to take.
